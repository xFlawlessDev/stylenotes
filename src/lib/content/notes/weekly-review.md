## Close the loop

Once a week, sit down for fifteen minutes and answer three honest questions.

1. **What moved forward?** Name the one thing that actually progressed.
2. **What stalled?** Notice what kept slipping without judging it.
3. **What deserves Monday?** Pick a single focus for the week ahead.

### Keep a small ledger

Gratitude, however small, compounds.

- A walk without a phone.
- A message you finally sent.
- A page you finally deleted.

### Reset the physical space

A tidy desk is a signal to your brain that the previous week has ended. Close the old tabs, clear the mugs, and start fresh.
