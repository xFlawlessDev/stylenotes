## Weeknight, no thinking

Some recipes exist purely to remove decisions. These are those.

### Ten minute miso noodles

- Noodles of any kind.
- One spoon of miso, loosened in hot water.
- A soft egg, or not.
- Whatever greens are wilting in the drawer.

### Charred broccoli bowl

1. Broccoli, olive oil, and a hot pan until the edges blacken.
2. A grain, a sharp dressing, and something crunchy on top.
3. Squeeze half a lemon. Serve in the pan you cooked it in.

### Notes for future cook

- Salt the water like the sea.
- Taste before serving, every single time.
- A recipe is a starting point, never a rule.
