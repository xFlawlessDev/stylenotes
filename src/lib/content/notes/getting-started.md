## Welcome to StyleNotes

StyleNotes is a quiet place for loud ideas. Everything lives on your machine, opens instantly, and stays out of your way while you write.

### Capture before you organize

Start every thought in the **Inbox**. Structure can come later. Use `#tags` freely, rename folders whenever a theme emerges, and never lose a sentence to a missing category.

1. Press **New Note** to open a blank page.
2. Write a title that you would actually search for later.
3. Add one or two tags to give future-you a trail.

### Folders that stay tidy

Folders are just a lens on your notes, not a filing cabinet. A note can move between *Work*, *Ideas*, and *Personal* without ever being duplicated or copied.

> A note is never lost. It is only waiting for the right moment to be found.

### Make it yours

Switch the density, tune the accent color, and let the interface recede. The goal is simple: the interface should disappear and your words should remain.
