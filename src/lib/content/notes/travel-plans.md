## Slower plans win

The best weekends are under-scheduled. This frame keeps the essentials and leaves the rest open.

### Friday

- Arrive before dark and walk the town once.
- Find dinner by following the warmest window.

### Saturday

- Wake without an alarm.
- A long trail in the morning, a book in the afternoon.
- One view worth waiting for at sunset.

### Sunday

- Slow breakfast.
- One small shop, one good coffee.
- Home before the evening rush.
