## A calm, material surface

The visual language borrows its structure from **Material Design 3** and its softness from translucent, layered glass. Depth comes from blur and light rather than hard borders.

### Spacing rhythm

Everything aligns to a **4px** baseline. Generous whitespace is treated as a first-class element, not as leftover room.

- Screens breathe with a 24px outer margin.
- Cards group related ideas and float above the canvas.
- The sidebar stays narrow so the writing area can lead.

### Color roles

Color is semantic, never decorative. Each token carries an intent:

- **Primary** marks the single most important action on screen.
- **Secondary** supports navigation and quiet accents.
- **Tertiary** highlights status and small confirmations.

### Depth and light

```
Layered glass, in three moves:
1. A soft blur on the surface behind.
2. A hairline highlight along the top edge.
3. A wide, low-opacity shadow to lift the panel.
```

When in doubt, remove one layer rather than adding a border.
